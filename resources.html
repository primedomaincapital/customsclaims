The courts

The rulings that made the refunds.

    The February 20, 2026 opinion itself: 6–3, holding that the International Emergency Economic Powers Act does not authorize the president to impose tariffs.

    supremecourt.gov

  • Justia — Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump

    The same case in a readable, citable form, with the docket and the opinions that joined the majority.

    steptoe.com
